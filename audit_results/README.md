# Historical audit results

The 15 reports in this directory are retained historical engineering-review
snapshots for the name-identity, bundle-analysis, extractor, and identity-item
work completed during the 2024–2025 development period and represented in the
repository's 2.1-era changelog.

This material is **non-operational**. It is not configuration, a runbook, a
test fixture, or runtime input, and production correctness does not depend on
it. Current behavior is defined by maintained contracts, policies, code,
operator documentation, tests, and the accepted decisions in `DECISIONS.md`.
Links among these reports and links from `CHANGELOG.md` are historical citations
only.
